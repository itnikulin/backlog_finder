## Prioritization rules

First classify each item as mandatory work, product opportunity, technical enabler/debt, or insufficiently evidenced hypothesis. Mandatory work is ranked separately from discretionary work.

For comparable discretionary items, score each factor from 0 to 5:

| Factor | Weight | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Outcome impact | 30% | Expected movement of a named product or business outcome |
| Customer reach | 15% | Share or number of target users affected |
| Urgency / delay cost | 15% | Time sensitivity and consequence of postponement |
| Evidence confidence | 15% | Strength of research, data, or validated learning |
| Risk reduction / enablement | 10% | Risk retired or future work unlocked |
| Effort efficiency | 15% | Value relative to delivery and operational effort |

Weighted score = sum of `(factor score / 5) × factor weight`. Report it as 0–100 and always show confidence separately.

### Guardrails

- A high score cannot override a mandatory legal or security obligation.
- Missing evidence lowers confidence; it does not silently become a zero-impact judgment.
- Do not compare raw scores across fundamentally different work classes without explanation.
- Prefer reversible experiments when impact is uncertain and learning value is high.
